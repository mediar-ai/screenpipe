Screenpipe can keep meeting transcription and summaries on your computer with local models. Fireflies offers cloud meeting notes, collaboration, and CRM integrations, with bot and botless capture options. The relevant privacy difference is where processing happens.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Screenpipe | Fireflies.ai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capture | Enabled microphone, system audio, and screen sources | Meeting bot, desktop app, or Chrome extension |
| Botless recording | Yes | Available through desktop or extension capture |
| Transcription and summaries | Local Whisper and local models available; cloud providers optional | Hosted meeting-processing workflow |
| Storage | Local history; optional sync and exports | Cloud storage; private storage options for eligible plans |
| Screen history | Permitted app context across the workday | Meeting-focused context |
| CRM output | Configured connections and tasks | Native CRM integrations |

## Private storage and local AI are different

Fireflies has private storage options and enterprise controls. Check where transcription and summaries run as well as where recordings are stored. A private storage bucket alone does not move that processing onto your laptop.

For Screenpipe, follow [private meeting notes](/private-meeting-notes) to choose local transcription and a local summary model, disable external processing and exports for that workflow, and verify a short offline sample. Cloud models and connected apps can send context off-device. Local model performance depends on your hardware and the task; hosted plans have their own usage limits.

## Choose based on the workflow

Fireflies may fit a team that wants shared meeting notes, conversation analytics, and native CRM sync. Consider Screenpipe if you need processing on your own computer, access to the source code, or screen history beyond meetings. Follow recording and consent requirements with either tool.
